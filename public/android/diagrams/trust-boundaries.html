Trust boundaries of an Android app

Use it when: asked for a threat model of an app handling sensitive data.

Say it like this

  1. Draw the boundaries first: your process, other apps and the OS, the network, the backend. Threats cross boundaries.
  2. Inbound edges are untrusted: keep content providers private unless needed, prefer explicit intents, validate every input including push payloads.
  3. Data at rest: choose what to store, encrypt it, keep keys in the keystore, never ship secrets in the APK.
  4. Data in transit: TLS with certificate validation; discuss pinning or transparency checks and the operational cost of rotating them.
  5. Logs and analytics are an outbound edge too: no personal or health data.
