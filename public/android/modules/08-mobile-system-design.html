The architect round: turn a vague prompt into requirements, a structure, and defended trade-offs in 45 minutes.

Why it comes up

  • It is where architecture, concurrency, security and delivery meet; interviewers watch your process more than your final diagram.

Know cold

  • A repeatable process (see the framework diagram): clarify requirements, list constraints (offline, security, scale, team), layers and modules, data and sync, security and privacy, testing and release, trade-offs and risks.
  • Principles to apply (R9): persistent data models drive the UI, single source of truth, unidirectional data flow, main-safe types, fresh data persisted for offline use.
  • On-device AI is alpha: per the third-party recap (R6), Gemini Nano 4 with the ML Kit Agentic API is alpha with listed platform requirements; treat as a proof of concept, not a production design.
  • Say the trade-off out loud: each decision gets a "because" and a "we give up".

Likely questions

Prompt What a strong answer contains
Design an offline-capable app for viewing and redeeming prescriptions. Local SSOT, sync strategy and conflict rules, security of data at rest, expiry and consent, failure states.
Design push notification handling and deep links. Delivery guarantees, payload minimization, authentication on open, idempotent handling.
Structure an app for ten teams. Module graph, ownership, API boundaries, build-time budget, CI.
Add an AI assistant feature. On-device vs cloud, privacy, fallback, cost, testing nondeterminism.
Migrate a legacy app to Compose and modules. Incremental plan, risk ordering, metrics, ADRs.

Practice

  • ☐ Do three timed 45-minute sessions on the prompts above; draw, speak, and record yourself.
  • ☐ After each, write what you would change; add one architecture story to the story bank.
