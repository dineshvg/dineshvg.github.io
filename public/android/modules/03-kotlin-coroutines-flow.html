03 · Kotlin coroutines and Flow

status: todo · priority P1 · 4h · verified 2026-11-16

Practice 15 flashcards

Concurrency questions separate people who use coroutines from people who understand cancellation, scopes and testing.

Why it comes up

  • A standard senior screen; bugs here (leaked scopes, swallowed cancellation) are expensive.

Know cold

From R11 and R12.

  • The ten Android practices: inject dispatchers; make suspend functions main-safe (use withContext inside); let the ViewModel create coroutines; do not expose mutable types; expose suspend functions and Flows from the data and business layers; inject test dispatchers; avoid GlobalScope; make coroutines cancellable; handle exceptions; keep learning.
  • Scopes: coroutineScope or supervisorScope for work tied to the screen; an injected external CoroutineScope for work that must outlive it.
  • Cancellation is cooperative: check ensureActive() before long blocking steps. Never swallow CancellationException; rethrow it if you catch it. Catch expected exceptions such as IOException inside coroutines in viewModelScope.
  • Cold flows run per collector: builders, operators, flowOn to change context, catch for upstream errors, channelFlow for concurrent emission.
  • Hot flows: SharedFlow and StateFlow; cold flows can be converted to hot ones; know how to cancel them and handle exceptions.
  • Testing: runTest with StandardTestDispatcher or UnconfinedTestDispatcher, sharing one scheduler.
  • UI collection: convert Flow to Compose State with collectAsStateWithLifecycle() (R13).

Likely questions

Question What a strong answer contains
Why inject dispatchers? Testability and one place to change threading; show the test replacement.
StateFlow vs SharedFlow vs a channel for one-off events? State has a current value and conflates; events must not be lost or replayed; state your choice and its failure mode.
What happens if you catch Exception around a suspend call? You can swallow CancellationException and break structured concurrency; rethrow or catch narrower types.
coroutineScope vs supervisorScope? Failure propagation: one child failing cancels siblings or not.
How do you make a CPU-bound loop cancellable? Cooperative checks such as ensureActive() or yield().
How do you test a ViewModel exposing a StateFlow? Injected test dispatcher, runTest, assert emitted states, no real delays.
Where do you launch work that must survive the screen? Injected application-level scope, not GlobalScope.
